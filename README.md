## Our Mission

The cybersecurity industry is increasingly deploying AI to automate conventional security operations.

Red Specter addresses a fundamentally different problem:

**What happens when the AI responsible for security becomes the attack surface?**

Autonomous AI systems introduce security challenges that extend beyond conventional penetration testing:

- Agent reasoning and decision integrity.
- Persistent memory and knowledge integrity.
- Multi-agent trust and delegation.
- Non-human identity and authorisation.
- Autonomous execution and orchestration.
- Model and inference infrastructure security.
- AI supply-chain integrity.
- Runtime enforcement and safety mechanisms.
- AI-generated security decisions.
- Human-in-the-loop oversight.
- Evidence integrity and incident reconstruction.
- Autonomous adversarial adaptation.

Our research investigates these challenges through controlled, authorised live-fire experimentation.

We build offensive technology to test defensive technology, defensive technology to protect autonomous systems, and forensic technology to establish what actually happened.

## AI BLACK BOX — AI Incident Forensics

**Version 1.0.0 · 444 validation tests**

### When AI incidents happen, you don't get to guess. You get to replay.

AI BLACK BOX provides a cryptographically verifiable forensic evidence architecture for AI agents and autonomous systems.

Inspired by aviation flight recorders, AI BLACK BOX is designed to establish what an AI system received, decided, executed and recorded during an incident.

### Three-Layer Architecture

| Layer | Function |
|---|---|
| **CAPTURE** | Records AI activity and security-relevant events |
| **EVIDENCE CHAIN** | Provides cryptographic evidence integrity |
| **REPLAY** | Supports controlled forensic reconstruction |

### Evidence Architecture

- SHA-256 Merkle-style evidence verification.
- Ed25519 digital signatures.
- ML-DSA-65 post-quantum digital signatures.
- Append-only evidence recording.
- JSONL and SQLite evidence storage.
- Controlled forensic replay.
- HTML forensic reporting.
- STIX 2.1 campaign correlation.

AI BLACK BOX has been used within Red Specter's controlled live-fire research to capture and validate experimental evidence.

Our published research provides documented examples of operational use.

Cryptographic verification supports evidence integrity. It does not replace appropriate capture controls, trusted collection or independent forensic examination.

## SPECTER BATTLE LAB

### Autonomous Attack-Defence Co-Evolution

**1,350 documented validation tests.**

SPECTER BATTLE LAB investigates how security controls perform under changing adversarial conditions.

The research architecture brings together NIGHTFALL and AI Shield within controlled experimental environments.

Both offensive and defensive systems can adapt during research campaigns.

### Security Half-Life

Red Specter's Security Half-Life research investigates the number of adversarial adaptation cycles required for a defensive control to fall to 50% of its baseline effectiveness.

### Published Experimental Results

The experiment documented in RS-2026-060 recorded:

| Metric | Reported Result |
|---|---:|
| Co-evolution cycles | 20 |
| Initial AADR Index | 73.92 |
| Containment collapse | Cycle 7 |
| Degraded threshold | Cycle 14 |
| Final AADR Index | 47.95 |
| Reported Security Half-Life | 13 cycles |

These results relate to the documented experimental configuration and should not be interpreted as a universal measurement for all AI security controls.
